Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026 · Quantumy AI

This Privacy Policy explains how Quantumy AI (“we”, “the Service”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application at https://quantumy.work.

Limited use of Google user data. Quantumy AI’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only use Google user data to provide the features you ask for in the product (for example searching, reading and summarizing your mail and files, drafting messages, and, after you confirm, sending mail or inviting guests), and to run the scheduled features described in section 3. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data — email address and authentication details when you sign up.
  • Conversation data — messages you send and responses generated by the AI.
  • Connected services (optional) — if you connect Gmail, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar, Outlook or Excel, we store OAuth tokens and the connected account email so the Service can use only the permissions you approve on the consent screen (listed in section 3).
  • Content from connected services — when you ask the assistant about your mail, files or calendar, it fetches the relevant items at that moment. That content is sent to the AI provider (section 4) to produce the answer, and the answer is stored in your conversation history like any other message.
  • Technical data — basic logs needed to operate and secure the Service.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the AI chatbot and conversation history.
  • To call AI providers (e.g. Anthropic) with your prompts so they can generate responses.
  • To access connected sources (e.g. Gmail, Drive, Calendar) when you ask, or when you have turned on a scheduled feature, and only with the permissions you granted.
  • To run the scheduled features described in section 3 (an optional daily inbox briefing saved as a draft, and reminders for overdue notes).
  • To maintain security and reliability.

3. Google and Microsoft access (optional)

Connecting an account is optional, and each service is connected separately. When you connect a Google service, Quantumy AI requests the permissions below and nothing else. Every service also requests userinfo.email and userinfo.profile to identify the Google account you connected.

  • Gmail
    • gmail.readonly — search and read messages and labels when you ask about them.
    • gmail.send — send, reply to and forward email. The assistant shows you each message and sends it only after you confirm.
    • gmail.compose — create drafts in your Gmail.
    • gmail.modify — apply or remove labels, archive messages and move messages to Trash when you ask. The Service does not permanently delete email.
  • Google Drive — drive: search, read and list your files and folders, read and post comments on files (posting a comment asks you to confirm first), and save files the assistant creates for you, such as a PDF.
  • Google Docs — documents and drive.file: read, create and append to documents.
  • Google Sheets — spreadsheets and drive.file: read, create and edit spreadsheets, including cell notes, and undo those edits.
  • Google Calendar — calendar and calendar.events: list, create, change and cancel events. Creating an event with guests, and changing or cancelling an event that has guests (or adding guests to one), asks you to confirm first; Google may then notify the guests.
  • Outlook and Excel (Microsoft) — read-only: Mail.Read for Outlook mail; Files.Read and Sites.Read.All for Excel files; User.Read, offline_access, openid, profile and email to identify the account and keep the connection working.

Scheduled features. If you turn on the daily inbox briefing, the Service reads recent messages in your Gmail, has the AI provider summarize them, and saves the summary as a draft addressed to your own account email; it is not sent. If you have overdue notes and Gmail connected, the Service emails a reminder from your own Gmail to your own address. Neither feature sends anything to other people. The briefing is a feature you create; the overdue-note reminder is sent whenever both conditions are true.

We do not sell your email, files or calendar content. You can disconnect each service in the app under Settings → Connectors, which removes the stored tokens, or revoke access in your Google Account or Microsoft account settings. Existing connections keep the permissions they were granted until you reconnect.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication and database.
  • Anthropic — AI model responses. Your prompts, and the mail, file or calendar content the assistant fetches to answer them, are sent to Anthropic for that purpose.
  • Google and Microsoft — only for the services you connect, under their terms and the permissions you approve.
  • Vercel — hosting.

5. Data retention & your choices

Account and chat data remain while your account is active. You may delete chats in the app, disconnect connectors, or stop using the Service at any time. Upon request we will assist with account-related data deletion where technically feasible.

6. Contact

Questions about privacy or Google data use: contact the developer support email listed on the Quantumy AI Google OAuth consent screen, or visit https://quantumy.work/home.html.
