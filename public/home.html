How we use Google user data

Transparent explanation of why Quantumy AI requests access to your Google account data.

What we request

If you choose to connect Gmail, Quantumy AI requests access to read your Gmail (gmail.readonly), to send mail you confirm (gmail.send), to create drafts (gmail.compose), and to label, archive and move messages to Trash (gmail.modify), plus your Google account email address and profile (userinfo.email, userinfo.profile). The assistant sends mail to other people only after you confirm it. Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Calendar are separate connectors with their own permissions, all listed in the Privacy Policy.

Why we request it

The purpose is so you can ask Quantumy, in plain language, to search, summarize, draft and organize your own email (for example: “What did Sara send last week?”, “Summarize unread messages about the invoice” or “Draft a reply to Sara”). Access is used only to fulfill requests you make in the chat and the scheduled features described in the Privacy Policy. We do not use Gmail data for advertising, selling, or training external models beyond generating the response you asked for.

Your control

Connecting Gmail is optional. You can disconnect at any time in the app under Settings → Connectors, which revokes access and removes stored tokens from our systems. You can also revoke access in your Google Account security settings.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy (same policy linked on the Google OAuth consent screen).

Privacy by design

API keys never live in the browser. Connectors are opt-in, and the assistant asks you to confirm before it sends mail, invites guests or comments on a shared file. You can revoke connectors at any time.
